About Urban Roadside

Urban Roadside | Battery & Key Rescue is a Johannesburg-based mobile roadside service. We help drivers who are locked out, dealing with flat tyres, dead batteries, or key and ignition issues. Our goal is simple, get you back on the road quickly and safely.

How to get help: WhatsApp us, choose a service, share your live location, then send a short summary.
